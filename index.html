FAQ

What is Supernova?

Supernova is an draft Hack Club YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) program where members build cool projects, log their hours, and level up through space-themed tiers while earning rewards to spend in shop.

What do I get?

You earn Energy Cores for every hour you build, and can redeem them in the hardware shop. Insane projects can unlock Comets, rare rewards, and access to secret items.

Rules to follow?

Keep your work AI-free and original! Log your time honestly and do the best you can on your projects.

How can I track time?

Use Hackatime to track software projects. Use Lapse and journals to keep track of hardware projects.

What kind of projects can I make?

Anything space-related is fair game! Go crazy, the sky's not the limit this time!

Need more help?
